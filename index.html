About Me

I am a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at NIT Goa, currently spending my final year at IIT Madras in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) through a student exchange program under an MoU between NIT Goa and IIT Madras.

At IIT Madras, I work in the SIMHA Lab and RISE Lab, continuing the AI4VLSI research I began during my internship. My current research explores whether an agentic AI framework can be developed or used for gate sizing in RTL-based VLSI design flows.

Alongside research, I am taking courses aligned with my interests: CAD for VLSI (focused on Physical Design), along with electives in Machine Learning and Security Testing.
